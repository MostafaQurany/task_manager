# User Stories & Role Dashboards

## 1. As a Client (Client Portal)
- I want to log in and see my company's specific dashboard containing my Contracts, Services, Projects, and Deliverables.
- I want to see the progress of my services (e.g., 3/5 Designs Approved) and project-based work (e.g., Mobile App 62% Progress).
- I want to review deliverables that are "Ready for Client Review" and choose to either **Approve**, **Request Changes**, or **Add Comment**.
- I want to view a full history of my requested revisions and the resulting new versions for each deliverable.
- I want to be completely isolated from the agency's internal communications, employee performance metrics, and technical task breakdowns.
- I want to submit a "New Request" directly from my portal, specifying the service, deadline, and attaching files.

## 2. As an Employee (e.g., Designer, Developer, Copywriter)
- I want to open my "My Tasks" dashboard and instantly see tasks categorized into: **Today**, **Ready to Start**, **In Progress**, **Blocked**, **Revisions Requested**, and **Waiting for Review**.
- I want to see clear dependency notices. If a task is blocked, I want to know exactly what is blocking it (e.g., "Blocked by: Content Approval" or "Blocked by: Backend API"), so I am not held accountable for the delay.
- I want to submit my completed work for "Internal Review" before it ever reaches the client.
- I want to see explicit "Client Comments" or "Internal Comments" on my tasks to understand requested revisions.

## 3. As a Team Lead
- I want a Team Dashboard showing: Tasks Ready, In Progress, Blocked, Revision Requests, Internal Reviews, Client Revisions, Late Tasks, and Upcoming Deadlines.
- I want to review my team's deliverables (Internal Review) and either approve them to proceed to the client or request internal revisions.

## 4. As an Account Manager
- I want to create and map Deliverables directly to specific Client Contracts and Services.
- I want to handle situations where a client requests work beyond their contracted quota by marking it as an "Extra Request" (Approve as Free Extra, Paid Extra, Add to Next Month, or Reject).
- I want to see exactly what is "Waiting on Client" (e.g., Waiting for Content, Information, Files, Payment Approval) vs "Waiting on Our Team".

## 5. As Management
- I want a bird's-eye view of all Active Clients, Current Contracts, Deliverables this month, Late Deliverables, Pending Approvals, Extra Requests, and Team Workload.
- I want detailed Internal Reports (Performance by Client/Team/Employee, Late Tasks, Revision Rates, Contract Usage) to identify bottlenecks.
